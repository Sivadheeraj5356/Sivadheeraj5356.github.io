01

Fixing a hang in Minigrid's Hilbert-curve heuristic above 16x16

upstream, mergedNov 2026

Farama Foundation's Minigrid is one of the standard RL benchmark environments. Its goal-position heuristic silently hangs on grids larger than 16x16. Traced it to the Hilbert-curve traversal ordering, wrote a reproduction, and patched the traversal so large grids resolve. Merged upstream the same day.

  • PR - Farama-Foundation/Minigrid#548
  • Follow-up - #549, spiral heuristic on non-square grids
