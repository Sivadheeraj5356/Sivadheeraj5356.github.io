Notes on human-AI interaction

What comes after the chat window

Ten essays on what interacting with intelligence could feel like beyond chat: agents with manners, voice that hears more than words, memory you can touch, and interfaces that know what they do not know.

Siva Dheeraj · November 2026 · 10 essays

  • 01
    Chat is the command line of AI

    Why the text box won, and why the future belongs to artifacts you can touch, not transcripts you scroll.

  • 02
    Software that knocks

    Proactivity is a privilege agents hold at the user's pleasure, and the penalty for bad manners is being muted.

  • 03
    The audit trail is the interface

    When AI works while you are not looking, the review screen becomes the whole product.

  • 04
    Voice is not a transcript

    Speech is the richest input we have, and the standard pipeline strips it for parts.

  • 05
    Generative UI should keep its landmarks

    Interfaces generated fresh for every task have a problem nobody prices in: nothing stays put.

  • 06
    Steering wheels, not prompts

    We gave agents engines and left them a destination field with no wheel, no mirrors and no brakes.

  • 07
    Memory you can touch

    An AI that remembers you is a relationship. An AI with a hidden file on you is surveillance. The difference is the interface.

  • 08
    Show me what you'd do

    Asking users to specify what they want is a tax. The best interfaces pay it for them.

  • 09
    The group chat is the hardest problem in AI UX

    One human and one agent was the tutorial. The real world is a room full of people.

  • 10
    Confidence is a visual problem

    Chat renders a guess and a certainty in the same font. That is a design failure, not a model failure.
