NOTES ON HUMAN-AI INTERACTION · 07 / 10

Memory you can touch

An AI that remembers you is a relationship. An AI with a hidden file on you is surveillance. The difference is the interface.

Today, memory is a dossier: invisible, unaccountable, uneditable. Most people discover the AI remembers them only when it surfaces a fact they do not remember giving, which is the creepiest possible way to find out.

Memory as objects

Every remembered fact should be a first-class object: inspectable, sourced, editable, expirable. "Why do you know this?" deserves a real answer - said on this date, in that conversation, in this context. If the answer cannot be produced, the memory should not exist.

Scopes are the missing social layer

What the AI may recall alone with you differs from what it may recall in front of your colleagues. Humans perform this filtering effortlessly and call it discretion. An agent without scopes has none, and a single discretion failure in a group setting undoes a year of trust.

Forgetting is a feature

Memory review should be routine hygiene, the way you review subscriptions. Most things said once should fade. The right to be boring - to have an ordinary remark not follow you forever - is part of what makes a relationship safe.

The real distinction is between a notebook about you and a relationship with you. A relationship accumulates context that both parties can see and shape. The dossier is one-way glass. Build the first.
